Frequently Asked Questions

What is today's 30-year fixed mortgage rate?

Today's average 30-year fixed mortgage rate is sourced from Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey (PMMS) and updated daily. Use the filter above to view 30-year rates from major US lenders.

What is the current Federal Funds Rate?

The Federal Funds Rate is set by the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) and directly influences mortgage rates. The current rate is displayed in the banner above, sourced from the Federal Reserve.

How do I calculate my monthly mortgage payment?

Use our free mortgage calculator above. Enter your home price, down payment, loan term, and interest rate to instantly see your estimated monthly payment broken down by principal, interest, property tax, and insurance.

What is the difference between a 15-year and 30-year mortgage?

A 15-year mortgage typically has a lower interest rate but higher monthly payments. A 30-year mortgage offers lower monthly payments but you pay more total interest. Click any rate card above to pre-fill the calculator and compare.

Which bank has the lowest mortgage rate today?

Rates vary daily. MortgageRate.live compares Chase, Wells Fargo, Bank of America, Citi, Rocket Mortgage, U.S. Bank, and the Freddie Mac national average. Sort by "Lowest rate" in the filter bar above to find today's best offer.
